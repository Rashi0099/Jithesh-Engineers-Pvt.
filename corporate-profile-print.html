CORE SPECIALIZATIONS

Engineering Disciplines & Standards

01. MULTI-STORY RCC & COMMERCIAL FRAMING

Full 3D dynamic analysis for high-rise residential towers and commercial plazas. Modeling column ductility, shear walls, wind load drift, and soil-structure interaction.

STANDARDS: IS 456, IS 1893, IS 13920, ACI 318
02. INDUSTRIAL STEEL & PEB STRUCTURES

Design and optimization of Pre-Engineered Buildings, clear-span warehousing, crane gantries, and industrial processing plants with material optimization.

STANDARDS: IS 800 (Limit State), MBMA, AISC 360
03. SPATIAL TRUSSES & SPACE FRAME CANOPIES

Specialized long-span tubular node space frames, stadium canopies, entry spatial trusses, and expressive structural architecture across India and Saudi Arabia.

STANDARDS: IS 800, Eurocode 3, Saudi Building Code (SBC)
04. DEEP FOUNDATIONS & GEOTECHNICAL MODELING

Bored cast-in-situ piles, pile cap groups, buoyant raft basements, diaphragm walls, and slope stability analysis for coastal and tricky soil stratas.

STANDARDS: IS 2911 (Pile Foundations), IS 1904
05. 3D BIM DETAILING & REBAR FABRICATION (BBS)

Clash-free 3D structural BIM models, structural drawings, bar bending schedules (BBS), and steel connection shop drawings minimizing site wastage.

TOOLS: ETABS, SAFE, STAAD.Pro, AutoCAD, Revit
06. STRUCTURAL AUDITS, NDT & RETROFITTING

Non-Destructive Testing (Rebound Hammer, Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity), distress mapping, core compression checks, and carbon fiber (FRP) wrapping schemes.

STANDARDS: IS 13311 (Part 1 & 2), IS 15988
